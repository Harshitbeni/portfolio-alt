Life is about to change. If things go according to plan, nothing that I know about my life will be the same in the next 3 months.

Today, I am sitting here in a quaint beach town in Kerala, India, in a shack and writing this. My first-ever blog post, it feels unnatural, crass, and as people my age like to call it, cringe.

I feel that big life changes are the perfect opportunities to start anew. Which is why I am starting this blog. I hope to write every week. I'm not sure what I will write, but the plan is to write anything. It can be just 5 words, but it is something.

Some people write to think better, and some people write to share their thoughts. I have always wanted to be a storyteller, in one form or another. Writing feels like the foundational base of good storytelling. If you can write a good story, you can narrate a good story, enact a good story, and present a good story.

This is my effort to become a good storyteller. Through writing.
