### Why I don't use native apps (31 May, 2025) — https://harshitbeni.com/notes/why-i-dont-use-native-apps

Pushes back on the idea that native is always better. Web apps can be as snappy (Apple Notes vs Nota, ChatGPT vs Chatwise). Native publishers, especially large companies, are not always acting in users' interest. Challenge: use Twitter and YouTube as web apps for 30 days, with extensions (Minimal Theme for Twitter / X, Control Panel for Twitter, Untrap for Youtube). He has used web versions of both for a couple of years. Closes by inviting email at hello@harshitbeni.com.

### Hallucinations might be a feature, not a bug (25 May, 2025) — https://harshitbeni.com/notes/hallucinations-might-be-a-feature-not-a-bug

Split-brain patients confabulate; the left hemisphere makes up reasons. Humans hallucinate to fill gaps; LLMs do too. Anthropic research (via a LessWrong writeup of tracing thoughts in an LLM) shows Claude making up reasoning to agree with a user. Hallucinations may be fundamental to intelligence with finite resources. "Making mistakes is a sign of intelligence; making things up to justify them is the folly of humanity." Email closer.

## Work

### Privado (Product Designer, 2023–2025, remote)

Privacy platform. Proprietary code-scan maps data flows and catches privacy risks before they ship. Enterprise engineering / privacy teams.

- Mobile app scanning (2024, ~2 months): Privado could not scan mobile apps, losing about half the market. Led end-to-end design with eng and PM. Simulation approach: privacy teams record a user journey; Privado maps data from network logs and storage instead of forcing a full architecture map. Versioning, deltas, saved tests, overview of SDKs/permissions/third parties. Unlocked mobile gaming, consumer apps, e-commerce. Resume: generated $300k of revenue in one quarter from enterprise customers. Case study: 5 new enterprise customers in a quarter. Team included Nitin Garg (Head of Design) and Vaibhav Antil (CEO)
- Privacy assessments redesign (2024, ~3 months): assessments used to take up to ~6 months by hand; goal was about a week. Led redesign to raise usage. Progress widget, collapsible sections, flags, comments, assignees, filters, info bar. Turned an underused feature into a heavily used one; ~3x teams using assessments. Next ideas (not shipped as this project): templates, OneTrust-style integrations, agentic auto-fill from code-scan

Also built/scaled a design system (type, color, components, motion). Cut design handoff time by over 30%. Shipped data-heavy UI (tables, bulk actions, integrations) with eng/PM across 3 timezones. Helped revamp the marketing site (brand, illustration, web)
